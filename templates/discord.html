Discord bot

The stv.tf voice bot brings a team's voice channel into a live match: delayed so it matches SourceTV, and stored in the demo.

Add to Discord

Once

  1. Add the bot to your server. It can stay there.
  2. Each player: /stv link, so their voice is matched to the right player in-game.

Each match

  1. Start the match on stv.tf.
  2. On its page: 🔊, Comms, Discord, Copy command.
  3. Paste it in Discord, while in your voice channel.

The command is for that match only, while it is live: comms cannot be added to a demo afterwards.

The bot shows that it records. It leaves when the channel is empty or the match ends. One voice channel per Discord server.

Commands

/stv joinJoins your voice channel, with the link from the match's page
/stv delayChanges the delay (the server's tv_delay, 90 by default)
/stv leaveLeaves
/stv linkLinks your Steam account

On Mumble: stv.tf/mumble.
