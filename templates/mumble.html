Mumble bots

The stv.tf voice bot records a team's channel for a match. It signs in by one of these certificates, the same every time; a second bot on a server at the same time, uses the second, and so on.

Check: right-click the bot, Information: its certificate's SHA-1 is in this list.

Register: have the bot join once (a match's Comms menu, Mumble), then right-click it, Register. It is the same bot from then on.

More than one team: register stv.tf-2 the same way, while two bots are in.

For scripts: mumble.json.
